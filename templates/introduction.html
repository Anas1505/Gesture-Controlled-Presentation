Gesture Controlled Presentation

Experience a whole new way of controlling your presentations using intuitive hand gestures. This system is designed to make presentations seamless, interactive, and futuristic.

Why Use Gesture Control?

  • 🚀 Hands-free navigation for a smoother experience
  • 🤖 Uses AI-powered hand tracking for accuracy
  • 🖥️ Works with any image-based presentation slides
  • 🎨 Allows real-time drawing and annotations
  • ⚡ No need for a clicker—just use your hand!

How It Works

  • 👋 Raise your pinky to move to the next slide
  • 👍 Show thumbs up to go to the previous slide
  • ✏️ Use your index finger to draw on the slides
  • 🖐️ Raise index, middle & ring fingers to undo the last drawing
  • 👊 Close your fist to clear all drawings
